About Limpeza Management Services

Fifteen Years of Uncompromising Quality

For over 15 years and counting, Limpeza Management Services Limited has stood as a pillar of reliability across Trinidad & Tobago. What began as a dedicated cleaning company has evolved into an all-in-one property management and maintenance solution provider.

From restoring delicate terrazzo and hardwood floors to emergency sewage line treatments, grease trap maintenance, high-pressure washing, electrical work, and full property oversight—our motto remains standard across every job: "Where our service beats the rest."
